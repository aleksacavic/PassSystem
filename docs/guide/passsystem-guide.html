Who does what

Pick your role

Everyone signs in at the same site. What you see depends on your role. Administrators run the whole system, and organizers and operators are assigned per event.

Whole system

Administrator

Runs PassSystem: creates events, brings people in, and decides who manages what.

  • Create, edit, publish, close and archive every event
  • Invite staff by email and remove accounts
  • Make or remove other administrators
  • Assign organizers and operators to events
  • Everything an organizer and operator can do
Create an event
  1. Sign in, then open Events → Create event.
  2. Fill in name, link name, date and time, time zone, venue and capacity.
  3. Set when registration opens and closes (optional).
  4. Check the Attendance terms. Edit the text if needed, or switch off required acceptance.
  5. Save, then press Publish when you're ready for guests.
Invite a staff member
  1. Open Staff in the top menu.
  2. Under Invite staff, enter their email. Tick Administrator only for people who should run everything.
  3. Press Send invite. They get an email, choose a password, and appear as Invite pending until they do.

The built-in email service sends only a few emails per hour. If you hit the limit, wait an hour.

Put someone on an event
  1. Open the event → Staff tab.
  2. Enter their email and choose Operator (scanning) or Organizer (full event management).
  3. Press Assign. The event shows up for them at once.
Remove someone
  1. Open Staff. For an administrator, press Remove admin first.
  2. Press Remove (or Cancel invite) and confirm.
  3. They lose access to every event. Their past check-ins stay in the records.

You can't remove yourself, and the last administrator can't be demoted.

Per event

Organizer

Manages one event end to end: the guest list, the door team and what happens on the night.

  • Edit event details, attendance terms and status
  • Search, revoke, restore and reissue guest passes
  • Export the guest list to CSV
  • Add operators to their event
  • See every scan in the Activity tab, and scan passes
Share the registration link
  1. Open the event. On Overview, find Share registration.
  2. Press Copy link and send it by message, email or social post.
  3. The Overview counters show registrations and check-ins live.
Handle a guest problem
  1. Open the event → Attendees, and search by name, email or phone.
  2. Reissue makes a new QR when a guest lost theirs. The old one stops working.
  3. Revoke blocks a pass. Restore undoes it if there's still room.
Close or wrap up the event
  1. Open the event → Edit → Status.
  2. Closed stops new registrations, and the door can keep scanning.
  3. Archived hides the event and keeps all data. Use Export CSV first if you need a copy.
Per event · door

Operator

Works the door with a phone. Sees the events they're assigned to and nothing else.

  • Scan passes for their events
  • See who was just scanned on their phone
  • Retry a scan when the connection drops
Scan guests in
  1. Sign in on your phone and tap Scan. Pick the event if you have more than one.
  2. Allow the camera when the phone asks.
  3. Hold the guest's QR inside the frame. The result fills the screen.
Read the result
  1. Green · ADMITTED: let them in.
  2. Red · ALREADY USED, INVALID, REVOKED, WRONG EVENT: don't admit. Send them to an organizer.
  3. Amber · NOT VERIFIED: the connection dropped. Tap Retry and don't admit until it turns green.

A retry never counts twice. If the first attempt went through, the retry confirms it.

No account needed

Guest

Opens the link, fills a short form, and carries the pass on their phone.

  • Register with name, and optionally email and phone
  • Read and accept the event's attendance terms
  • Keep the pass on the phone, as a screenshot, or in Google Wallet
Get a pass
  1. Open the link from the organizer.
  2. Enter first and last name. Email and phone are optional. Phone needs the country code, like +971 50 123 4567.
  3. Open and read the terms, tick I have read and accept the attendance terms., then submit.
  4. The QR pass appears. The same link opens it again later on this phone.
Keep it safe
  1. Take a screenshot. The door reads it like the live pass.
  2. On Android, tap Add to Google Wallet.
  3. Don't share it: the first person to scan it gets in.

Good to know

The small print

Attendance terms

Every new event starts with a UAE-oriented template covering non-political gatherings, open invitation, conduct, photos and personal data. Guests must tick it before a pass is issued, and the exact text they accepted is recorded. Have a lawyer review it before real events.

Phone numbers

Optional for guests, always with the country code: +971 50 123 4567 is saved as +971501234567. Numbers without + are refused.

Capacity & windows

When an event is full or registration is closed, the form says so and no pass is issued. Revoking a pass frees its place.

Lost pass

An organizer presses Reissue in Attendees and sends the new link. The old QR stops working at once.

Google Wallet

Android guests can save the pass to Google Wallet with the same QR. It's in test mode until Google approves publishing, so only listed test accounts can save for now.

Security

Passes are long random codes, and the system stores only a fingerprint of each one. Every scan is checked on the server, so screenshots can't be reused and a copied QR still works only once.
